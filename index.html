About the conference

Why it matters

Boys learn what a man looks like from the men they meet. The Positive Role Models Conference puts 500 senior secondary school boys in one room with exemplar male CEOs who built businesses, organisations and communities the right way.

The theme, WIN RIGHT, is simple: success counts when it is earned with character. Boys leave with a clearer picture of who they can become, and with real people they can look up to.

Entry is free. Seats are limited to 500 boys, so registration is required.

Frequently asked questions

Who can attend?

Senior secondary school boys (SS1 to SS3). Seats are limited to 500 boys, and each boy must register with a parent or guardian's details.

Is it free?

Yes. Entry is free, lunch included. Registration is required.

What should he bring?

Your registration ID (on your phone or printed), a pen and a notebook. Leave valuables at home.

What about parents and guardians?

A parent or guardian must give consent when registering and will receive the confirmation by email and WhatsApp. To ask about accompanying your son, call or email us.

Will lunch be served?

Yes. Lunch is part of the programme. Tell us about any allergies or dietary needs in the registration form.

Is there a dress code?

Come neat and comfortable, in smart-casual clothes.
